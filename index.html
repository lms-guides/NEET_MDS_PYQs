MDS PYQ Bank

Source: a memory-based compilation of NEET MDS 2021, 2022 and 2025 papers. NBEMS publishes no official key, so answers here are unofficial and a few are wrong; questions flagged “Check the key” are ones I found contradictory. Subjects were tagged automatically, so some may sit in the wrong subject. The 2025 questions come with answers only. 2023 and 2024 are not included because the source lists only topics for them.

Import, export and reset

Reset one subject

Clears your attempts and bookmarks for that subject, then gives its options a fresh random order.

Import questions

Paste a JSON list. ans is the correct option number starting at 0.

[{"subject":"Oral Pathology","year":2023,"q":"Question text","opts":["A","B","C","D"],"ans":1,"exp":"Why B is correct"}]
Export questions

Shows the questions you imported as JSON so you can copy them out.
